Current Research Interests

  • Network analysis of brain connectomes in neurological and psychiatric disorders — graph-theoretical analysis of connectivity and topological properties; cognitive aging and degeneration focusing on cognitive reserve, brain flexibility, plasticity, and resilience
  • Machine learning for biological data, particularly brain neuroimaging — disease classification, super-resolution, and segmentation of neuroimages; deep learning-based quantification and prediction of graph-theoretical metrics; brain network decomposition and feature extraction (motifs, subgraph patterns)
  • Estimating glymphatic function in aging and dementia — non-invasive quantification of glymphatic clearance using multi-parametric MRI (IVIM, ePVS, DTI-ALPS); digital biomarkers for sleep architecture and glymphatic activity via wearable sensors
